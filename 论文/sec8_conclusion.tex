\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We developed a unified observe-deploy-reschedule framework for coordinated vehicle
localization of UAV alerts over a built 100-m field, and answered all four questions
with a shared geometry engine:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Q1.} The three-alert observable regions are $5628.7/6432.5/5484.8\,
\mathrm{m}^2$ (T1/T2/T3, single components, $\le0.23\%$ refinement drift), with
mission-cycle-minimizing stations at $(83.32,62.53)$, $(10.02,78.23)$ and
$(58.21,4.09)$ for the designated vehicles V4/V3/V2, each with slew $=$ reset
$=2.667\,\mathrm{s}$ and the $0.10$-m clearance margin of Assumption~7.
\item \textbf{Q2(1).} Joint RMSE $=\sigma_b\sqrt{d_1^2+d_2^2}/\sin\gamma$ with exact
lower bound $0.14362\,\mathrm{m}$---attained once the operating margins are relaxed
to zero; the deployed margin-compliant pair is V3+V5
($\mathrm{RMSE}=0.1461\,\mathrm{m}$, $+1.73\%$; total travel $19.88\,\mathrm{s}$),
validated by Monte Carlo to $0.0\%$ deviation.
\item \textbf{Q2(2).} The min--max schedule starts all alerts by
$11.046\,\mathrm{s}$ (makespan $21.705\,\mathrm{s}$) via one shared-station doubling
(V3: $T_1\!\to\!T_3$, zero-travel second leg), beating the greedy baseline by
$5.352\,\mathrm{s}$ ($32.6\%$); the honest gap to the per-alert lower bound is
$7.17\,\mathrm{s}$ ($6.78\,\mathrm{s}$ against the cross-implementation refined
incumbent, a certified $3.6\%$ station-level residual).
\item \textbf{Q3.} The pointing-error chain caps single-leg travel at
$s_{\max}=66.92\,\mathrm{m}$ for $95\%$ reliability (exact crossing $67.09\,\mathrm{m}$
by quadrature, so the analytic cap is conservative by $0.17\,\mathrm{m}$); at the
stated 80-m approach the reliability is only $91.54\%$, so reliability,
not range, binds deployment.
\item \textbf{Q4.} Event-triggered re-optimization restores full coverage in all 86
disturbed scenarios (worst $t_{\mathrm{last}}=33.75\,\mathrm{s}$), the original plan
absorbs only outages $\le10\,\mathrm{s}$ untouched, and the explainable greedy rule is
a certified $\le4.53\,\mathrm{s}$ slower fallback.
\end{itemize}

Operationally, the framework converts into three field rules: place station pairs at
right angles to the alert (Q2(1)'s $\gamma=90^\circ$ law); keep every vehicle's
cumulative leg under $\approx67\,\mathrm{m}$ (Q3's reliability budget); and trigger a
re-solve on every disturbance except a pure outage shorter than ten seconds (Q4's value
boundary). The model layer is deliberately conservative, so every schedule reported
here can be executed as printed.
